Who We Are

Welcome to Pastel Boutique 🌸

At Pastel Boutique, we celebrate elegance, youthfulness, and charm through our handpicked accessories. Launched in 2024, we stay true to our values of quality, affordability, and unique style.

We're passionate about delivering high-quality products that make every girl feel special. Our goal is to keep customer satisfaction our #1 priority while offering designs that are soft, charming, and modern.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

🌱
Early 2024

The Idea Was Born

A simple passion for pastel aesthetics and accessories turned into a vision — to create a boutique that every modern girl would love.

🛍️
Mid 2024

First Products Listed

We launched with 20 handpicked products — hair bands, rings, and earrings. The response from our first customers was overwhelming.

💜
Late 2024

500+ Happy Customers

Word spread fast. Within months we had hundreds of loyal customers who loved our quality and pastel aesthetic.

✨
2025

Expanded Our Collection

We grew to over 500 products across jewellery, hair accessories, earrings and bracelets — all staying true to our pastel vision.

🚀
2026

1200+ Customers & Growing

Today Pastel Boutique serves customers across Pakistan. We're just getting started — new collections drop every single week.
